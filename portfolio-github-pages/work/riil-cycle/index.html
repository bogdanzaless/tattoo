Context and task

The tariff's idea of choosing only what you use could be extended into a practical sustainability project.

Creative idea

Connect RIIL with Собиратор through a collection drive and a short eco quiz.

How it worked

Participants donated unwanted items, completed the quiz and could enter a prize draw. The partner action and the tariff message supported the same behaviour.
