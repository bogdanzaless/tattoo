Красный огонёк

A New Year music show for a young audience.

2025RealizedContributed to the creative concept and project format.

Context and task

РИИЛ needed a New Year format that felt relevant to a young audience without repeating a conventional seasonal advertising campaign.

Creative idea

Rework the familiar TV variety-show format as a contemporary live music project for different generations.

How it worked

The project combined performers from different generations, a live show, offline zones and content for social media. The same format worked as an event and as a set of publishable episodes and fragments.

My role

Contributed to the creative concept and project format.

Project status

Realized in 2025.
