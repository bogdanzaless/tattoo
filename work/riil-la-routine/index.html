Context and task

The fashion collaboration needed a digital continuation that could keep people involved after the product announcement.

Creative idea

Turn the bag frozen in ice into a playable object and make speed part of the collaboration.

How it worked

In the Telegram game, users tried to free the bag from virtual ice faster than others and hold their position in the ranking.
