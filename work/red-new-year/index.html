ПреКрасный Новый год

An interactive New Year mechanic for MTS RED.

RealizedCreative idea, mechanics and copywriting.
10

Context and task

MTS RED needed a simple seasonal mechanic that would give people a reason to return to the Telegram channel throughout the day.

Creative idea

Make a changing holiday wish the interface of the giveaway and hide one rare Gift version among the regular stickers.

How it worked

The wish changed every ten minutes. Users followed the sticker in the Движитал Telegram channel, looked for the rare version and could enter the draw when it appeared.
